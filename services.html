How To Book a Ride

Get from search to seat in under 60 seconds.

1

Search Your Route

Enter your pickup point, destination, date, and number of seats needed. Our smart algorithm instantly finds available rides on your route.

2

Pick Your Driver

Browse verified driver profiles, ratings, reviews, and vehicle details. Choose the driver you feel most comfortable with and send a booking request.

3

Ride & Enjoy

Get pickup confirmation, track your driver in real time, enjoy the ride, and pay automatically through the app. Then leave a review for your driver!

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing is calculated based on distance, route demand, and the number of passengers sharing the ride. Drivers set their own base price per seat, and our platform suggests a fair range. You'll always see the total price before confirming your booking — no hidden fees, ever.
Your safety is our top priority. All drivers go through a thorough background check, driving license verification, and vehicle inspection. Both drivers and passengers build public profiles with ratings and reviews. Every ride includes live GPS tracking, and our emergency SOS button connects you directly to our safety team and local authorities.
Every driver's profile shows their verification badge, star rating, total trips completed, and community reviews. You can also see their vehicle make/model/license plate before confirming. A "Verified" badge means they've passed our background check, ID verification, and driving record review.
Yes, you can cancel up to 2 hours before the scheduled departure with a full refund. Cancellations within 2 hours of departure may incur a small fee to compensate the driver. Drivers can also cancel, and you'll be notified immediately with a full refund. Frequent cancellations may affect your trust score.
Sign up for a free account, then navigate to "Offer a Ride" and begin the driver onboarding. You'll need to upload your driver's license, vehicle registration, and insurance documents. Our team reviews submissions within 24–48 hours. Once approved, you can start posting rides immediately!
If your driver doesn't show up, use the "Report No Show" button in the app. You'll receive a full refund within 3–5 business days, and our team will investigate the driver. We take reliability very seriously — repeated no-shows result in permanent account suspension.
