Pricing FAQ

Pricing Questions

Absolutely! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately. If you downgrade, you'll continue to enjoy your current plan until the end of the billing period, then switch to the new plan.
Yes! Both Standard and Premium plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. At the end of the trial, you'll be prompted to enter payment details to continue. If you don't, you'll be automatically moved to the Free plan.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. For corporate accounts, we also support wire transfers and invoicing on a monthly basis.
If you exceed your monthly ride limit on the Free or Standard plan, you can still book rides at a small per-ride fee ($1.99/ride for Free, $0.99/ride for Standard). We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit so you can decide whether to upgrade or pay per extra ride.
We offer a full refund if you cancel within the first 7 days of a new billing period. After 7 days, cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle and no partial refunds are issued. Annual plans that are cancelled within 30 days of purchase receive a prorated refund.
