Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RideFlow?

RideFlow is a local car pooling and ride share platform for everyday commuters. It connects riders heading the same way with drivers who have empty seats, so they can share the trip and split the cost.

How do I find a ride?

Open Find a Ride and enter your pick-up point, destination, and travel date. You can compare available seats, price per seat, departure times, and verified driver ratings before you choose.

How do I offer a ride and earn?

Use Offer a Ride to share your route and departure time. Riders can then book the empty seats in your car, and you set the price per seat.

How are payments and payouts handled?

Seats are paid securely online when a rider books, and drivers receive their earnings for every seat they share.

How does RideFlow keep the community safe?

Every member completes identity and driving checks, riders and drivers rate each other after each trip, and an in-app emergency button helps you share your live location with trusted contacts.

Can I manage a trip after booking?

Yes. Open My Bookings in your dashboard to review your upcoming and past trips, and manage your rides from there.
